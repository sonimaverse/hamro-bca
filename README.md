## 🌟 Overview

Hamro BCA is more than a notes website.

It is a centralized digital ecosystem designed to support BCA students throughout their entire academic journey.

Whether you're looking for lecture notes, past questions, syllabus PDFs, lab manuals, faculty resources, community notes, or academic announcements, Hamro BCA brings everything together in one organized platform.
